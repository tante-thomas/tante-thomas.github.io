      Music for body and soul.

      TANTE THOMAS is a queer DJ from Hamburg playing music for body and soul. With house at heart, Tante cares less about genre than about where the music takes you. Sometimes that means losing yourself in the groove, sometimes closing your eyes and letting a track sting a little. Music to move, feel, let go and be somewhere else for a while.
